Seeing and smelling

TDT16 (XR), our WebXR talk. On a Quest 3, open it in the Quest Browser and press Enter VR. On a computer, use the arrow keys (add ?emulate for an emulated headset).

  • The whole talk: the billboard, then the coffee
  • Smell only, or from the rendering pipeline
  • The billboard only

In VR: A next, B back, X the step's sources, hold Y (left controller) for a second to leave VR.

Presenting: the goggles' view on the laptop

Both devices open this site; the goggles send their head pose and every button press to the laptop over WebRTC (PeerJS only introduces them). Both need internet for the first handshake.

  1. Laptop: open the spectator and share that window. It shows a code, the same one every time in this browser.
  2. Goggles: type the code here, open the talk, Enter VR. The dot in the corner turns green on both.
